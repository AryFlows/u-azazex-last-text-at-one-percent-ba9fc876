The funniest final text a dying phone sends at one percent battery.

## The entry (131 characters, 1 line)

> 1%. Before I die, a confession: I never liked your ringtone, your password is embarrassing, and yes, I saw the photos. Delete them.

## Why this line

A deathbed confession in the phone's own voice: it opens on "1%", settles
three scores before it dies (the ringtone, the password, the photos), and
ends on a demand — "Delete them." No generic battery warning, no "charge me"
pun; the joke is the phone having opinions and secrets, and the photos are
the surprise that makes it laugh out loud.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`
(`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` overrides the output directory for local runs).
